Quote it is built local-first: every client, catalog item, estimate, and invoice you create lives on your device by default. This policy explains the little that ever leaves it — and exactly when that happens.

01Overview

Quote it is an offline-first quoting and invoicing app for solo contractors and small trade crews. The app works fully without an account. Signing in is optional and only needed if you want cloud backup, multi-device sync, or to share a business with up to five crew members.

This policy covers what happens in both cases — signed out (fully local) and signed in (optional sync).

02Information we collect

CategoryExamplesWhere it lives
Business data you enter Clients, catalog items, estimates, invoices, line items, payments, business profile, logo Device only
Account data Email address, password (handled by Firebase Authentication — never stored in plain text by us) Cloud, if signed in
Synced business data The same clients/catalog/invoices above, mirrored for backup and crew sharing Cloud, if sync used
Crew & invite data Crew member email, role, and a short-lived invite code (expires after 7 days) Cloud, if crew used
Purchase data Subscription status for Quote it Pro, handled entirely by Apple/Google — we never see your payment details App Store / Play

We do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or usage tracking of any kind. There are no third-party ad or analytics SDKs in Quote it.

03How we use it

  • To generate the PDF estimates and invoices you create, including your business logo and signature.
  • To back up your business data and restore it if you sign in on a new device.
  • To let an invited crew member see the same clients, catalog, and jobs as the business owner.
  • To unlock Quote it Pro features after a verified purchase or restored subscription.

We never use your data to train models, sell it, or share it for advertising.

04Storage & retention

Default — signed out

All data is stored in a local SQLite database on your device. Nothing is transmitted anywhere. Deleting the app deletes this data permanently.

Optional — signed in with sync

Business data is mirrored to Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, hosted by Google, under a Google Cloud project we control. Sync happens on your action — app open, saving a document, or tapping "Sync Now" — never continuously in the background.

05Sharing with others

We do not sell or rent your data. It is shared only with the service providers required to run the app:

  • Google Firebase — authentication and optional data sync/backup.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — processing in-app subscription purchases.
  • Crew members you invite — see the same business's clients, catalog, and documents once they redeem your invite code. Removing a crew member revokes their access.

06Security

Cloud data is protected by Firestore security rules that restrict every read and write to members of your business — no one outside your crew can access your data, even other Quote it users. Authentication is handled by Firebase Auth; we never see or store your password.

07Your choices

  • Use Quote it without an account — sync is opt-in, never required.
  • Delete your local data — uninstalling the app removes everything stored on-device.
  • Delete your cloud data — contact us (below) and we will delete your account and associated business data from Firebase.
  • Leave a crew — a crew member can be removed by the business owner at any time from the app's Crew screen.

08Children's privacy

Quote it is a business tool for contractors and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

09Changes to this policy

If we change how Quote it handles your data, we'll update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the update.
