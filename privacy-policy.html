Invoice Maker is local-first: the clients, catalog items, estimates and invoices you create are stored on your device. This policy explains what leaves your device, and when.

01Overview

Invoice Maker is an offline-first estimate and invoice app for contractors and small crews. It works fully without an account. Signing in is optional and only needed for cloud backup, using the same business on more than one device, or sharing a business with up to five crew members.

This policy covers both cases: signed out (everything stays on your device) and signed in (optional sync).

We do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, location, or usage tracking of any kind. Invoice Maker contains no advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

03Permissions

  • Photos: only when you choose a business logo. You pick one image; the app does not read the rest of your library.
  • Camera: never used. The photo picker library includes camera support, but Invoice Maker never opens the camera.

04How we use it

  • To create the estimate, invoice and receipt PDFs you share, with your logo and the client's signature.
  • To back up your business data and restore it when you sign in on another device.
  • To let crew members you invite see the same clients, catalog and documents.
  • To unlock Invoice Maker Pro after a purchase or a restored subscription.

We never sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

05Storage and retention

Signed out (default)

All data is stored in a database on your device and nothing is sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Signed in, with sync

Your account is stored in Firebase Authentication and your synced business data in Cloud Firestore, both run by Google in a Google Cloud project we control. The app syncs when you open it while signed in and when you tap "Sync Now"; it does not sync in the background. Cloud data is kept until you delete your account.

06Sharing with others

We do not sell or rent your data. It is shared only with the services needed to run the app:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore): sign-in, backup and sync.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play: subscription purchases.
  • Crew members you invite: they see the business's clients, catalog and documents after they redeem your invite code. Removing a crew member ends their access.

When you share a PDF, you choose the app and the recipient (email, WhatsApp, SMS and so on); we are not involved.

07Security

Data is sent to the cloud over encrypted connections. Firestore security rules allow only members of your business to read or change its data, and only someone with a valid invite code can join it. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication.

08Your choices and deleting your data

  • Use Invoice Maker without an account. Sync is optional.
  • Delete your account in the app: Settings > Manage Account & Sync > Delete My Account. This permanently deletes your account and your cloud data. If you own the business, its cloud copy is deleted for your whole crew. Data on your device stays there until you delete the app.
  • Delete data on your device: delete the app.
  • Leave a crew: the business owner can remove a crew member at any time from the Crew screen.
  • Ask us: email us (below) and we will delete your account and cloud data for you.

More detail: Delete your data.

09Children's privacy

Invoice Maker is a business tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.
