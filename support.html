02Getting started

  • Add your business details: Settings > Business Profile. Your name, logo, address, tax rate, currency and payment terms appear on every PDF.
  • Create an estimate or invoice: on the Dashboard, tap New Estimate or New Invoice, pick a client, and add items from your catalog or type your own.
  • Get it signed: open the document, tap Preview & Share PDF, then Capture Signature. A signed estimate is marked approved.
  • Send it: on the same screen tap Share PDF, then choose email, WhatsApp, Messages or any other app.

03Frequently asked questions

Does it work without internet?
Yes. Everything is stored on your phone. You only need internet to sign in, sync, buy a subscription or share a PDF through an online app.

How many documents can I make for free?
5 new estimates or invoices each calendar month. The count resets on the 1st. Invoice Maker Pro removes the limit.

What does Pro include?
Unlimited estimates and invoices, all 5 PDF templates, no app credit on your PDFs, analytics (monthly revenue, estimate win rate, top clients) and crew sharing with up to 5 crew members, who join for free.

How do I restore my subscription on a new phone?
Sign in to the same Apple ID or Google account, open the Analytics tab, tap Upgrade to Pro, then Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription?
On iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play > Payments & subscriptions. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel it.

How do I use my data on another device?
Sign in on both devices with the same account (Settings > Sign In / Sign Up). On the new device the app finds your business and offers to switch to it.

How do I add my crew?
Settings > Manage Crew > Create Invite Code, then send the code. Your crew member signs in, opens Manage Crew and enters it under Join a Crew. Joining is free, and crew members get Pro features while they are on a Pro owner's crew. Pro includes up to 5 crew members besides you. Codes expire after 7 days.

How do I change the currency?
Settings > Business Profile > Currency, then Save Business Profile.

How do I delete my account?
Settings > Manage Account & Sync > Delete My Account. See Delete your data.
